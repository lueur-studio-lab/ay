在巴黎，拾起那些被遺落的瞬間

契機

2年前搬來巴黎之後，日子好像一直在尋找方向——住處、實習、工作，每一件事都在流動。後來有了一段被迫暫停的日子，與其說是放假，不如說是一個不知所措的等待期。照常和朋友出門、逛展覽、聊心情，卻總覺得少了點什麼。

手機裡存著咖啡廳的午後光線、展覽裡人來人往的片段、巴黎街頭的隨性瞬間。在和幾位知心的前輩聊完天後，某個夜晚，那個開關突然被啟動了。說是突然，其實早有伏筆——碩士課程裡接觸過網頁設計，去年也曾動念，只是沒有真正落實，就這樣擱著。這一次，終於做了。

初衷

社群的消耗，好像比自己以為的還要來得重。能夠建立一個屬於自己的空間，是一份小小的成就感，也是一本記事，記錄著那些巴黎的點滴，還有在歐洲生活的平凡與不平凡。

對你說

或許你會在某篇裡找到一點共鳴，也說不定。

Picking up what Paris leaves behind

Two years after moving to Paris, life still felt like a constant search for direction — finding a place to live, figuring out internships, then work. Everything kept shifting. A while back, I found myself in a stretch of forced stillness. Less a holiday, more a waiting period with no clear end in sight. I still went out with friends, visited exhibitions, talked about how things felt — but something was missing, and I couldn't quite name it.

My phone was full of it, though: afternoon light in cafés, the quiet drift of people through gallery halls, the unhurried randomness of Parisian streets. After conversations with a few mentors I deeply admire, something clicked one evening. It felt sudden, but looking back, it had been building — I'd studied web design during my master's, thought about doing this last year, never followed through. This time, I did.

Why not just post on Instagram?

Because I wanted somewhere quieter. Somewhere the algorithm couldn't reach. The weight of social media had been heavier than I'd realised, and building a space that was entirely my own felt like a small but meaningful thing. A place to keep the everyday moments of life in Paris, the ordinary and the not-so-ordinary.

A note to you

Maybe you'll find something that resonates. Maybe you won't. Either way, you're welcome here.
